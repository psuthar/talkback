## Best order to review materials

For a reviewer who has *not* attended any interview:

1. Skim `documents/Senior_Staff_Engineer_JD.pdf` (~2 min) — anchors expectations.
2. Skim `documents/Alex_Chen_Resume.pdf` (~2 min).
3. Read `documents/Interview_Feedback_Packet.pdf` (~5 min) — see the scorecard split before the transcripts so you spot the disagreement instead of forming it yourself.
4. Watch / skim transcript of `final_hiring_committee_debrief` (~12 min) — captures the live disagreement.
5. Pull specific transcripts as needed when claims surface — most reviewers will only need to dive into 2 of the 7.
6. Use TalkBack Q&A to settle specific factual questions ("Did Alex describe leading the migration or contributing to it?").

For a reviewer who already attended one round, the value of TalkBack is steps 4–6: surfacing what happened in the rounds they missed, with citations.
